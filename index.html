HOW TO PLAY

  1. Take turns placing your 9 pieces on empty points. Three of your pieces in a line (including the diagonals) forms a mill and removes one opponent piece.
  2. Once all pieces are placed, take turns sliding a piece to a connected empty point. Forming a new mill again removes an opponent piece.
  3. When you're down to 3 pieces, you can fly to any empty point instead of just an adjacent one.
  4. A player loses when reduced to 2 pieces, or when they can't make a legal move on their turn.
